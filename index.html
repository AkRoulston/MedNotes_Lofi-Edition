1. Cardiac Cycle & Pressure-Volume Loops

The cardiac cycle comprises ventricular systole and diastole, strictly regulated by electrical depolarization starting at the Sinoatrial (SA) node.

Clinical Vignette Presentation:
A 58-year-old female presents with crushing chest pain radiating to the left jaw. Troponin I levels are elevated. ECG displays ST-segment elevation in leads II, III, and aVF.

Left ventricular end-diastolic volume (LVEDV) determines the preload, directly altering stroke volume via the Frank-Starling mechanism.
